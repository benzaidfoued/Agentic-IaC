# Community code of conduct

We aim for a welcoming, professional project. Be considerate, give constructive technical
feedback, credit contributions, and respect different backgrounds and experience levels.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexual harassment and sustained personal
attacks are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Contact a repository maintainer privately to report concerns. Maintainers will review reports
confidentially as far as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and take proportionate action:
a reminder, content removal, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion. A person involved
in a report should not decide its outcome. Appeals can be sent privately to another maintainer.
Repository owners must publish a private contact method before opening the project publicly.
